# Pro reports

* **Show New Results**: The results that are new against the baseline, with level, rule, location and message, and how many are gone.
* **Show Suppression Audit**: Every suppression with its rule, location, kind, status and justification, for audits.

The first Pro command starts a free 14-day trial on this computer. After the trial, Pro is a one-time purchase: USD 19 (Personal) or USD 39 per user (Business).
